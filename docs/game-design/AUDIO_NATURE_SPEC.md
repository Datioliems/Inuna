# Audio And Nature Spec

## Audio

- Always keep a quiet base ambience so the world never feels silent.
- Add area ambience zones for village, river, and forest.
- Use fade margins around each zone so sound changes are gradual.
- Footsteps change by surface tag: dry ground vs shallow water in V1.
- All V1 audio is placeholder/procedural until original licensed sounds are recorded or sourced.

## Nature

- Birds are a small system, not hand-placed static props.
- Water uses animated shader noise plus foam-like bands.
- Trees sway lightly to support calm, not spectacle.
- Healing quest completion should shift world feeling through color, NPC dialogue, and audio state.
